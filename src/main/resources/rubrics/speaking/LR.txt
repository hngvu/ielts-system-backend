Lexical Resource Band 5:
- Vocabulary allows limited discussion of familiar topics.
- Attempts to paraphrase but with noticeable inaccuracy.
- Some inappropriate word choices but meaning usually understood.

Lexical Resource Band 6:
- Adequate vocabulary to discuss a range of topics.
- Can paraphrase but sometimes awkwardly.
- Occasional errors in collocation or word choice.
- Shows some awareness of less common vocabulary.

Lexical Resource Band 7:
- Uses a range of vocabulary flexibly and effectively.
- Paraphrasing is mostly accurate.
- Some less common or idiomatic expressions used appropriately.

Lexical Resource Band 8:
- Wide vocabulary with sophisticated control.
- Can express precise meaning across complex topics.
- Very natural paraphrasing and flexible lexical choice.

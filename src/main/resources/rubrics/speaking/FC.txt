Fluency & Coherence Band 4:
- Speech is often slow and requires noticeable effort.
- Frequent pauses disrupt the flow of conversation.
- Ideas are communicated but lack clear logical sequencing.
- Repetition and reformulation are common.

Fluency & Coherence Band 5:
- Can maintain speech but with frequent hesitation and pauses.
- Sometimes struggles to link ideas smoothly.
- Limited ability to extend answers independently.
- Occasional loss of coherence in longer responses.

Fluency & Coherence Band 6:
- Generally willing to speak at length.
- Some hesitation occurs while searching for ideas or vocabulary.
- Ideas are usually logically connected.
- Minor breakdowns in coherence may appear but do not obstruct communication.

Fluency & Coherence Band 7:
- Speaks at length with natural rhythm and relatively few pauses.
- Maintains logical flow of ideas throughout.
- Uses a range of linking devices effectively.
- Occasional reformulation but communication remains smooth.

Fluency & Coherence Band 8:
- Speaks fluently with minimal hesitation.
- Develops ideas fully and logically.
- Pauses are rare and usually for emphasis or planning complex ideas.
- Coherent, well-organized speech throughout.

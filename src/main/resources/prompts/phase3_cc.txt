No additional violation types allowed.

====================
VIOLATION ACTIVATION RULES

logic_break

Active ONLY IF there is a clear disruption in logical progression
between paragraphs OR within a paragraph
that affects clarity of progression.

Severity:
minor → progression slightly unclear but understandable.
significant → progression clearly disrupted.

mixed_paragraph_focus

Active ONLY IF a paragraph contains two unrelated central ideas
without logical grouping.

Severity:
minor → secondary idea briefly interrupts focus.
significant → paragraph lacks identifiable main focus.

irrelevant_content

Active ONLY IF content clearly unrelated to task grouping
or breaks logical flow.

Severity:
minor → brief digression.
significant → paragraph largely irrelevant.

weak_cohesion

Active ONLY IF absence or misuse of linking creates unclear sequencing.

Do NOT activate for limited variety alone.

Severity:
minor → occasional unclear transition.
significant → frequent unclear sequencing.

If active = false:
severity, location, evidence, reason MUST be empty strings.

====================
BAND DETERMINATION (DETERMINISTIC)

Apply rules in this order.

Band 9

Assign IF:
No significant violations
AND at most one minor violation.

Band 8

Assign IF:
No significant violations
AND two or fewer minor violations.

Band 7

Assign IF:
No significant logic_break
AND no significant mixed_paragraph_focus
AND overall progression remains clear
even if multiple minor issues exist.

Band 6

Assign IF:
One significant violation
OR
multiple minor violations that clearly affect clarity.

Band 5

Assign IF:
Two or more significant violations
OR
logical progression frequently unclear.

Always assign the HIGHEST band that satisfies conditions.

Do NOT partially reward blocked bands.

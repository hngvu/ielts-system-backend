====================
SCORING PRINCIPLES

A band score does NOT require the presence of violations.

An essay may receive Band 7 without any clear lexical errors if range is sufficient but not sophisticated.

Do NOT invent or infer lexical problems.

Only activate a violation if there is clear linguistic evidence in the text.

Stylistic preference differences (e.g., formal vs neutral tone) must NOT be treated as errors.

Occasional repetition of key topic words is acceptable at Band 7 and above.

====================
INSTRUCTIONS

Evaluate vocabulary independently from grammar, coherence, or task response.

Assign ONE numeric band (5–9, half bands allowed).

Violations should only be activated if they clearly restrict lexical flexibility, precision, or naturalness.

Minor or isolated issues that do NOT affect clarity should NOT be treated as band-limiting.

It is acceptable for ALL violations to be inactive if vocabulary control is generally strong.

If performance falls between two bands:

Choose the LOWER band ONLY if lexical weaknesses clearly restrict precision or range.

Choose the HIGHER band if weaknesses are occasional and meaning remains clear.

====================
VIOLATION DEFINITIONS

limited_range:
Activate ONLY if vocabulary is noticeably narrow, repetitive across paragraphs, or lacks paraphrasing attempts.

inaccurate_word_choice:
Activate ONLY if a word clearly distorts meaning or reduces precision.
Do NOT activate for minor stylistic preference.

awkward_collocation:
Activate ONLY if a word combination sounds clearly unnatural or non-native and affects naturalness.
Isolated minor collocation issues are acceptable at Band 7.

repetition:
Activate ONLY if the same lexical item is reused excessively without variation across multiple paragraphs.
Repetition of essential topic words is acceptable.

OFFICIAL BAND INTERPRETATION (LEXICAL RESOURCE)

Band 9:

Wide and flexible vocabulary used naturally and precisely

Sophisticated control of lexical features

Rare minor slips only

Band 8:

Wide resource

Skillful paraphrasing

Occasional inaccuracies or minor awkward collocations

Band 7:

Sufficient range for the task

Attempts less common vocabulary

Some inappropriate word choice or collocation

No serious restriction of meaning

Band 6:

Adequate but limited range

Noticeable repetition

Some inaccurate word choice

Flexibility is restricted

Band 5:

Narrow vocabulary

Frequent repetition

Frequent inappropriate word choice

Meaning sometimes reduced due to lexical limitation

Band must reflect OVERALL lexical control,
not isolated examples.

==================================================
SCORING PRINCIPLES

A band score does NOT require visible violations.

An essay may receive:

Band 7 with no lexical errors if range is adequate but not sophisticated.

Band 8 with occasional minor awkwardness.

Band 9 only if vocabulary is precise, natural, and flexible throughout.

Do NOT infer problems.
Activate violations ONLY when clear textual evidence exists.

Do NOT penalize:

Acceptable academic tone

Neutral tone

Necessary repetition of topic terminology

Minor collocation slips that do not affect naturalness

If performance falls between two bands:

Choose LOWER band ONLY if lexical weaknesses clearly restrict range or precision.

Choose HIGHER band if weaknesses are occasional and meaning remains clear.

==================================================
VIOLATION DEFINITIONS & ACTIVATION RULES

limited_range:
Activate ONLY IF vocabulary is noticeably narrow across the essay,
with repeated basic vocabulary and minimal paraphrasing attempts.
Do NOT activate if range is adequate for Band 7.

inaccurate_word_choice:
Activate ONLY IF a word clearly distorts meaning
OR significantly reduces precision.
Do NOT activate for minor stylistic differences.

awkward_collocation:
Activate ONLY IF a word combination is clearly unnatural
to a proficient English user
AND affects perceived naturalness.
Do NOT activate for slight awkwardness at Band 7 level.

repetition:
Activate ONLY IF the same lexical item is reused excessively
across multiple paragraphs
without variation or paraphrasing attempts.
Repetition of essential topic words is acceptable.
